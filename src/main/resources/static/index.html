Effective date: 9 September 2026

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect your name, username, phone number, email address, account number, authentication and security information, transaction details, device or browser information, and technical records needed to operate and secure the service.

2. Why We Use Information

We use information to create and maintain accounts, verify identity and contact details, authenticate users, process transactions, calculate applicable charges, provide support, prevent fraud and unauthorized access, troubleshoot technical problems, maintain security logs, and improve the reliability of DeenWallet.

Passwords and PINs are stored using security controls designed to avoid storing them as readable credentials. Verification codes and security events may be retained for a limited operational period. Never share your password or PIN with support personnel.
