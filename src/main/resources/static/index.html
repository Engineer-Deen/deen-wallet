DeenWallet Transfer Terms and Conditions

Effective date: 24 October 2026

These Terms and Conditions govern your use of DeenWallet Transfer, a digital wallet service that helps registered customers convert money between supported mobile-money services, send money to bank accounts, buy WAEC result pins and manage related account activities. By creating an account, you agree to these terms.

1. Eligibility and Account Registration

You must provide accurate, current information and keep your account details up to date. You are responsible for protecting your password, session PIN, phone and email access. One person must not create or operate accounts using another person's identity or credentials.

2. Verification and Security

We may require email verification, security checks, transaction confirmation, or other controls before allowing access to account features. We may temporarily restrict access after repeated incorrect credentials, suspicious activity, or security events. You must contact support if you believe your account has been compromised.

3. Identity Verification (KYC) and Daily Limits

Every account starts with standard daily limits on mobile-money conversions and bank transfers. The limits that currently apply to you are shown in the app. To raise them, you can verify your identity by sending the details and photos of a valid National ID card, driver's licence or passport, together with a selfie, from Verify your KYC in the menu. You must submit only your own genuine, valid and unaltered document, and the details you enter must match it. We review each request and may approve it, decline it with a reason, or later withdraw an approval if the information proves wrong, the document expires, or security or legal reasons require it. When a verification is withdrawn the standard limits apply again. A document can be verified on one account only. WAEC result pin purchases are not subject to the daily limits. We may change the limits and the documents we accept from time to time, and a transaction above your current daily limit will not be accepted.

4. Transactions and Mobile-Money Services

Transactions are processed according to the information you submit and the availability and rules of the relevant mobile-money provider or bank. You are responsible for confirming recipient details, including phone numbers and bank account numbers, before submitting a transaction, and a transaction sent to a wrong recipient cannot be reversed by us. Provider delays, outages, reversals, fees, or service restrictions may affect a transaction.

5. Failed or Delayed Transfers

If your payment is received but the transfer to the recipient cannot be completed, the money is held by DeenWallet Transfer while our team either retries the transfer or refunds the amount you paid to the number you paid from. We will tell you by notification and email. Contact support and quote your transaction reference if you need help.

6. WAEC Result Pins

WAEC result pins are digital codes supplied to you through DeenWallet Transfer. After a successful purchase the pin and serial number are sent to your email and can also be found in your notifications for a limited time (currently 30 days), so save a copy. Keep them private: anyone who has the pin and serial number can use them. Contact support quickly if a pin does not arrive.

7. Trusted Devices and Sign-in

Each phone or browser you sign in on is listed under Trusted Devices in the menu. You should review the list regularly and remove any device you do not recognise or no longer use; a removed device is signed out and must log in again. If you see a device you do not recognise, remove it, change your password and contact support. Biometric login, where available, uses the fingerprint or face recognition of your own Android device. You must enable it only on a device that holds your own biometrics, and you are responsible for any person whose biometrics are registered on a device you have enabled it on.

8. Prohibited Use

You must not use DeenWallet Transfer for fraud, impersonation, money laundering, unauthorized access, abuse of another person's account, manipulation of transaction records, submitting false, forged or someone else's identity documents, or any unlawful activity. We may suspend or restrict accounts where misuse is suspected or confirmed.

9. Fees and Charges

Applicable service fees will be presented by the system before a transaction is confirmed where supported. Third-party mobile-money and bank charges may also apply.

10. Notifications and Communications

We send you service messages by in-app notification, push notification and email, for example transaction receipts, refund updates, identity verification results, security alerts and WAEC pins. Please keep your email address accurate and your notifications enabled so that you do not miss them.

11. Availability and Service Changes

We aim to keep DeenWallet Transfer available and reliable, but uninterrupted service cannot be guaranteed. We may maintain, improve, suspend, or change features when reasonably necessary for security, operations, compliance, or product development.

12. Account Suspension and Closure

An account may be restricted, locked, deactivated, or closed when required for security, violation of these terms, suspected fraud, or operational or legal reasons. Where appropriate, we may provide a recovery or support process.

13. Limitation of Responsibility

To the extent permitted by applicable law, DeenWallet Transfer is not responsible for losses caused by incorrect information supplied by you, compromised credentials, a device left signed in, unsupported provider failures, network outages, or actions taken outside the DeenWallet Transfer service.

14. Privacy

Your use of DeenWallet Transfer is also governed by our Privacy Policy, which explains how account, identity verification and transaction information is collected, used, protected, and retained.

15. Updates

We may update these terms as the service changes. The effective date shown on this page identifies the current version. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised terms where applicable.

16. Contact

For account, security, identity verification or transaction support, use the support options provided inside DeenWallet Transfer.

DeenWallet Transfer Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DeenWallet Transfer handles information provided when you register, authenticate, verify your identity, use wallet features, contact support, or complete transactions.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect your name, username, phone number, email address, account number, authentication and security information, transaction details, saved recipient details, notification settings, the devices and sessions you sign in with, and technical records needed to operate and secure the service. If you choose to verify your identity we also collect the identity information described in section 3.

2. Why We Use Information

We use information to create and maintain accounts, verify identity and contact details, apply the correct daily limits, authenticate users, process transactions, calculate applicable charges, send receipts and service notices, provide support, prevent fraud and unauthorized access, troubleshoot technical problems, maintain security logs, meet legal obligations, and improve the reliability of DeenWallet Transfer.

3. Identity Verification (KYC) Information

When you apply for identity verification we collect the type of document you use (National ID card, driver's licence or passport), the full name on the document, the document number, your date of birth, the expiry date where the document has one, photos of the front and, where applicable, the back of the document, and a selfie. We use this only to confirm who you are, to set your daily limit, to stop the same document being used on more than one account, and to prevent fraud and meet legal obligations. The document photos and the document number are stored encrypted, and the number is also kept as a one-way fingerprint used to detect the same document on another account. Only authorised administrators can open them, and each time a reviewer opens your details or a photo it is recorded in an audit log. We do not show your documents to other customers and we do not sell them. If a request is declined the photos are deleted immediately and you may apply again. Approved verification information is kept while your verification is active and for as long as we reasonably need it for security, dispute handling and legal obligations.

4. Devices and Sign-in Sessions

For each sign-in we keep a record so that you can see and control where your account is signed in: the type of device and app or browser, the first and most recent time it was used, and the beginning of the network address it connected from. You can see these records under Trusted Devices in the menu and remove any of them, which signs that device out. Removed and expired sessions are cleaned up automatically.

5. Passwords, PINs, Biometrics and Security Data

Passwords and PINs are stored using security controls designed to avoid storing them as readable credentials. Verification codes and security events may be retained for a limited operational period. Never share your password or PIN with support personnel. If you turn on biometric login, your fingerprint or face data stays on your Android device and is never sent to or stored by DeenWallet Transfer. We store only a public key and a device label that let your device prove it is yours.

6. Transaction Information

Transaction records may include amounts, recipient details, provider or bank information, transaction status, reference codes, timestamps, and related technical records. This information is used to complete transactions, investigate disputes, maintain accurate records, apply daily limits, and protect the service.

7. Notifications

We send service notifications by email and, if you allow them, push notifications to your device. For push notifications we keep the device notification token supplied by your phone or browser. You can turn push notifications off in your device settings, but some important emails and security notices will still be sent.

8. Service Providers

DeenWallet Transfer may rely on infrastructure, email, database, payment, bank or mobile-money service providers to deliver features. Information necessary for a requested service, such as the recipient and amount of a transfer, may be processed by those providers under their applicable terms and privacy practices.

9. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not treat your personal information as public information. We may disclose relevant information when necessary to provide requested services, protect DeenWallet Transfer and its users, investigate fraud or abuse, comply with legal obligations, or respond to lawful requests.

10. Data Protection

We use technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the service, including authentication controls, access restrictions, encrypted credential and identity document storage, security logging, audit logs for access to identity documents, and controlled administrative access. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

11. Retention

We retain account, transaction, security, and support information for as long as reasonably necessary for service delivery, security, dispute handling, legal obligations, and legitimate operational purposes. Identity documents of declined requests are deleted immediately, and sign-in session records are removed automatically after they expire. Your transaction history, notifications and WAEC pin records are deleted automatically after a limited period (currently 30 days), except for transactions that are still being resolved, such as a refund that has not been completed. Identity verification (KYC) information is not part of this clean-up and is kept as described in section 3.

12. Your Responsibilities and Choices

You should keep your contact details accurate and promptly report unauthorized activity. You can review and remove your signed-in devices, turn biometric login on or off, and manage notification permissions on your device. Where the system provides profile or account controls, you may update permitted information, and you may contact support about information that requires administrative assistance.

13. Children

DeenWallet Transfer is intended for users who are legally permitted to use the service. We do not knowingly design the service to collect information from children who are not permitted to register.

14. Policy Changes

We may revise this policy as DeenWallet Transfer develops or legal and security requirements change. The effective date will be updated when a new version is published.

15. Contact

For privacy, security, identity verification, account, or transaction questions, contact DeenWallet Transfer support using the support options available in the application.
