DeenWallet Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DeenWallet handles information provided when you register, authenticate, use wallet features, contact support, or complete transactions.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect your name, username, phone number, email address, account number, authentication and security information, transaction details, device or browser information, and technical records needed to operate and secure the service.

2. Why We Use Information

We use information to create and maintain accounts, verify identity and contact details, authenticate users, process transactions, calculate applicable charges, provide support, prevent fraud and unauthorized access, troubleshoot technical problems, maintain security logs, and improve the reliability of DeenWallet.

3. Passwords, PINs and Security Data

Passwords and PINs are stored using security controls designed to avoid storing them as readable credentials. Verification codes and security events may be retained for a limited operational period. Never share your password or PIN with support personnel.

4. Transaction Information

Transaction records may include amounts, recipient details, provider information, transaction status, reference codes, timestamps, and related technical records. This information is used to complete transactions, investigate disputes, maintain accurate records, and protect the service.

5. Service Providers

DeenWallet may rely on infrastructure, email, database, payment or mobile-money service providers to deliver features. Information necessary for a requested service may be processed by those providers under their applicable terms and privacy practices.

6. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not treat your personal information as public information. We may disclose relevant information when necessary to provide requested services, protect DeenWallet and its users, investigate fraud or abuse, comply with legal obligations, or respond to lawful requests.

7. Data Protection

We use technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the service, including authentication controls, access restrictions, encrypted credential storage, security logging, and controlled administrative access. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Retention

We retain account, transaction, security, and support information for as long as reasonably necessary for service delivery, security, dispute handling, legal obligations, and legitimate operational purposes.

9. Your Responsibilities and Choices

You should keep your contact details accurate and promptly report unauthorized activity. Where the system provides profile or account controls, you may update permitted information or request support for information that requires administrative assistance.

10. Children

DeenWallet is intended for users who are legally permitted to use the service. We do not knowingly design the service to collect information from children who are not permitted to register.

11. Policy Changes

We may revise this policy as DeenWallet develops or legal and security requirements change. The effective date will be updated when a new version is published.

12. Contact

For privacy, security, account, or transaction questions, contact DeenWallet support using the support options available in the application.
